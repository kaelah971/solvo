# Solvo precision-instrument elevation

## Scope

Elevate the existing landing hero and its immediate execution strip without changing the product identity, lamp asset, lower landing content, routes, or backend behavior.

## Hero composition

- Preserve the approved pendant lamp image, dimensions, cable, and responsive placement.
- Add a stationary, soft cone of light beneath the bulb, confined to the hero wordmark stage. The rays must visibly illuminate the letters directly beneath the lamp without washing the entire page.
- Keep the large SOLVO typing loop. Add a full, faint, stationary SOLVO behind it so partial typing states still read as an intentional complete brand composition.
- Add a second stationary illumination layer on the center of the large letters so the lamp appears to affect the word itself.
- Keep the small spaced SOLVO centered and static beneath the main word.
- Tighten vertical spacing so lamp, wordmarks, descriptor, CTA, and strip read as one instrument panel.

## Primary action

- Add one restrained outline `OPEN SOLVO IN TELEGRAM` action beneath the descriptor.
- Use the existing Telegram configuration behavior and hide the configuration note in the hero.
- On short landscape viewports, hide the hero CTA to prevent overlap with the execution strip.

## Interaction states

- Navigation and mobile menu controls transition muted gray to off-white and reveal a centered 1px underline over 180–200ms.
- Enabled outline CTAs brighten the border, gain a barely visible charcoal tint, and translate upward by 1px. Disabled actions remain stationary.
- Execution-strip cells become real anchor links to the corresponding lower sections. Hover/focus brightens number/title and reveals a thin top indicator line. The strip remains one continuous surface, not three cards.
- Decorative hero arrows stay non-interactive and receive no hover treatment.
- Focus-visible states match hover language and remain clearly visible.

## Motion and accessibility

- Use only opacity and transform for hover movement. The small stationary ray/light treatment may use static gradients and masks.
- Preserve the existing typing rhythm and lamp breathing.
- Reduced motion removes typing, lamp breathing, entry staging, CTA translation, underline expansion, and strip indicator motion while keeping all content visible.
- Rays and ghost layers are decorative and hidden from assistive technology.

## Responsive verification

- Verify 800x600, 1920x1080, 390x844, and 844x390.
- Prevent hero content/strip overlap and horizontal overflow.
- Preserve mobile menu behavior and visible focus.
